What is mhosFIT?

mhosFIT is a free online image tool that gets your pictures ready for application forms. Crop, rotate a full 360°, resize and compress a passport size photo, a stamp size photo or a signature to the exact pixel size and KB limit a website asks for. It works smoothly on mobile, tablet and desktop, and your files never leave your device.

How to resize your photo or signature

  1. Choose Photo, Passport size, Stamp size, Signature or Custom.
  2. Select an image from your phone or computer, or drag it in.
  3. Drag, pinch to zoom, or use the sliders to move, zoom and rotate 360° until it fits the frame.
  4. If needed, turn on Remove background or pick a background colour.
  5. Tap Download to save a correctly sized, compressed JPG.

Frequently asked questions

What is mhosFIT?

mhosFIT is a free browser tool that crops, rotates, resizes and compresses your photo or signature to the exact pixel size and file size that an online form asks for.

What is passport size photo in pixels?

A standard passport size photo is 35 x 45 mm, which is 413 x 531 pixels at 300 DPI. Pick Passport size in mhosFIT, fit your face in the frame and download a JPG.

What is stamp size photo in pixels?

A stamp size photo is usually 20 x 25 mm, which is 236 x 295 pixels at 300 DPI. Pick Stamp size in mhosFIT and download a ready JPG.

How do I make a photo 300x300 pixels under 100KB?

Open the Photo tab, add your picture, position and zoom it inside the square frame, then tap Download. mhosFIT outputs a 300x300 JPG and compresses it automatically to stay under 100KB.

How do I make a signature 300x80 pixels under 60KB?

Open the Signature tab, add a photo of your signature on white paper, fit it in the frame and tap Download. The result is a 300x80 JPG under 60KB.

My form asks for a different size or KB limit. What should I do?

Use the Custom tab. Enter any width, height and maximum file size, then crop, rotate a full 360 degrees and download.

Can I remove or change the photo background?

Yes. After adding your photo, turn on Remove background and pick a colour such as white or blue. It works best on plain, evenly lit backgrounds and runs fully in your browser.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser, so your photo and signature never leave your device.
